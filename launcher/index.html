Dumpling LauncherHome screen prototype for the OnePlus 5T running postmarketOS. The same file runs full screen on the phone.

How it works

  1. The wallpaper has no buttons. Tap anywhere and an orb appears there.
  2. Press the orb and swipe towards an app. Let go to open it. A tap on the orb leaves the ring open for tapping instead.
  3. In an app, swipe up from the bottom edge to go home.
